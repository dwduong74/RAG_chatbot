## Nội dung chi tiết:

📢
CALL FOR PARTICIPATION – VIETNAM ELECTRONIC DESIGN CONTEST (VEDC 2026)

💡 Are you passionate about hardware design, embedded programming, and smart IoT systems?

💡 Are you looking for a nationwide academic competition where you can showcase your creativity and engineering skills?

🔥 The
Vietnam Electronic Design Contest (VEDC 2026)
is officially launched with the theme:

🤖
“EMBEDDED AI FOR SMART IoT SYSTEMS”

This competition provides an excellent opportunity for students to apply their technical knowledge, enhance research capabilities, foster innovation, and develop teamwork skills through the design and implementation of Embedded AI solutions for IoT applications.

👥
ELIGIBILITY

Undergraduate and college students from institutions across Vietnam.

Each team consists of
2–5 members
.

Each team may submit
one project idea or product
.

🏆
AWARDS

🥇
1 First Prize:
VND 20,000,000 + Certificate

🥈
2 Second Prizes:
VND 10,000,000 each + Certificate

🥉
3 Third Prizes:
VND 6,000,000 each + Certificate

🏅
4 Consolation Prizes:
VND 3,000,000 each + Certificate

⭐
3 Special Awards:
VND 1,000,000 each + Certificate

📅
APPLICATION DEADLINE:
June 30, 2026

🎯 Students of the
Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 (FEEE), Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT)
are highly encouraged to participate, transform innovative ideas into real-world solutions, and proudly represent PTIT at this prestigious national competition.
