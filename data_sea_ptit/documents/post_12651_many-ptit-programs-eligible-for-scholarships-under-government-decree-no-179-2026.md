## Nội dung chi tiết:

📢
MANY PTIT PROGRAMS ELIGIBLE FOR SCHOLARSHIPS UNDER GOVERNMENT DECREE No. 179/2026/ND-CP

According to
Government Decree No. 179/2026/ND-CP
, which establishes scholarship policies for students pursuing fundamental sciences, key engineering disciplines, and strategic technologies, several flagship academic programs at the
Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT)
are expected to be eligible for this special support scheme.

The Decree aims to foster the development of high-quality human resources in strategic technology sectors, contributing to Vietnam’s national digital transformation agenda, digital economy growth, and global technological competitiveness.

As one of Vietnam’s leading universities in engineering and technology, PTIT currently offers a wide range of programs aligned with the strategic technology fields prioritized by the Government.

🎓
Eligible Programs at PTIT

Integrated Bachelor–Master’s Talent Programs

Students admitted to the following integrated programs are eligible for scholarship support:

Information Security

Computer Science

Information Systems

Electronic Engineering

Telecommunications Engineering

(These programs have been officially approved by the Ministry of Education and Training.)

Semiconductor Integrated Circuit Program

Students admitted to the Semiconductor IC Design Program are also expected to benefit from the policy (currently awaiting official approval from the Ministry of Education and Training).

Undergraduate Programs in Priority Disciplines

The following PTIT programs are expected to be included in the priority list under Decree 179/2026/ND-CP (subject to final confirmation by the Ministry of Education and Training):

💻
Computer Science and Computing

Computer Science (7480101)

Computer Networks and Data Communications (7480102)

Artificial Intelligence (7480107)

🔐
Information Technology

Information Technology (7480201)

Information Security (7480202)

⚡
Electrical, Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering

Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering (7520207)

Control and Automation Engineering (7520216)

💰
Scholarship Benefits

One of the most significant highlights of Decree 179/2026/ND-CP is the provision of direct financial support for students pursuing strategic technology disciplines.

Scholarship levels include:

🎓
Undergraduate Students

From
VND 3.7 million to VND 5.0 million per month

🎓
Master’s Students

From
VND 5.5 million to VND 7.4 million per month

🎓
Doctoral Candidates

Up to
VND 8.4 million per month
, particularly for talent programs, semiconductor IC programs, and selected strategic technology disciplines.

This scholarship policy not only helps reduce financial burdens on students but also serves as a strong incentive for talented learners to pursue careers in critical technology sectors that are essential to Vietnam’s future development.

The inclusion of numerous PTIT programs in the scholarship scheme further reinforces the Institute’s position as a leading institution for high-tech workforce development while creating greater opportunities for study, research, and career advancement in the digital era.
