## Nội dung chi tiết:

KIST School Announces Spring 2027 Student Internship Program for PTIT Students

KIST School, affiliated with the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST), has announced the opening of applications for the KIST School Student Internship Program – Spring 2027. The program is available to students from partner universities, including the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT).

The program offers research-oriented students an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a research and development (R&D) environment at KIST, participate in research activities, and explore opportunities for pursuing Master’s and Ph.D. degrees at KIST School (UST).

According to KIST School, PTIT students are eligible to apply under the category designated for students from Vietnam and Indonesia.

1. Program Overview

The KIST School Student Internship Program is designed for students who intend to pursue Master’s or Ph.D. studies at KIST School (UST). It also provides opportunities to gain practical research experience and participate in R&D activities within KIST’s research environment.

During the internship, students will have opportunities to:

Participate in national research and development projects funded by the Korean government and relevant agencies and organizations;

Receive research guidance and mentorship from KIST researchers;

Access KIST’s fully equipped laboratory facilities;

Attend a Korean language training program;

Participate in academic and cultural activities organized for students.

2. Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements established by KIST School, including:

Maintaining their enrollment status at their current university throughout the entire internship period;

Having fewer than three semesters remaining before graduation as of October 6, 2026;

Being prospective candidates for KIST School (UST) graduate programs beginning in the Fall 2027 admission cycle or subsequent admission cycles.

3. Internship Period and Activities

Internship period: March 1, 2027 – July 31, 2027.

During the internship, students will participate in laboratory research activities, research and development (R&D) training, and language education.

Participation in the Korean language training program is mandatory.

4. Benefits and Financial Support

Selected students will receive support in accordance with KIST School’s policies, including:

Monthly financial support:
KRW 1,200,000 for undergraduate students and KRW 1,400,000 for graduate students, before tax deductions;

Dormitory accommodation:
Approximately KRW 180,000 per month;

Comprehensive insurance coverage;

Opportunities to participate in academic and cultural activities;

Research mentorship from KIST researchers and access to KIST’s laboratory facilities.

5. Application Documents

Applicants are required to prepare their application documents according to KIST School’s guidelines.

Initial Application Documents

Academic transcript:
Required;

Recommendation letter from an academic advisor:
Optional.

According to KIST School’s instructions, all required documents must be submitted through the online application system before the application deadline.

After the initial application screening, qualified candidates may be asked to submit additional documents, including:

A copy of their passport;

A curriculum vitae (CV), with no prescribed format.

6. Application Timeline and Selection Process

Step 1. Online Application

Application period: October 6–20, 2026

Students interested in the program should complete the online application and submit all required documents through KIST School’s application system by October 20, 2026.

Step 2. Application Screening

Following the application deadline, KIST School will review the submitted applications and forward those of qualified candidates to partner institutions for the nomination process.

The screening and nomination process is expected to begin on
November 3, 2026
.

Step 3. Candidate Nomination

Expected period: November 3–10, 2026

After KIST School completes its initial screening, PTIT will conduct the candidate nomination process in accordance with KIST School’s requirements.

According to the guidelines, each partner institution may nominate a maximum of 10 candidates.

Under this year’s revised nomination procedure, KIST School is expected to require an official recommendation letter issued in the name of the head of the institution or the Dean of the relevant faculty for nominated candidates.

This official recommendation letter forms part of the institutional nomination process and is separate from the optional recommendation letter from an academic advisor submitted during the initial application stage.

Step 4. Interview

Expected period: November 12–16, 2026

Nominated candidates will participate in online interviews conducted by the KIST School interview committee.

Step 5. Announcement of Results

Expected date: December 2, 2026

KIST School will announce the final selection results to applicants and partner institutions.

Step 6. Visa Procedures

Following the announcement of the final selection results, KIST School will provide guidance on visa applications and entry procedures for the Republic of Korea.

Further details regarding the schedule and required procedures will be announced later.

7. Application Instructions

PTIT students interested in the program are encouraged to review the program information, prepare the necessary documents, and submit their applications through KIST School’s online application system.

Program:
KIST School Student Internship Program – Spring 2027

Eligible applicants:
PTIT students who meet the program’s eligibility requirements.

Online application:

https://kist.re.kr/kist_school_eng/StudentTrainingProgram.do (https://kist.re.kr/kist_school_eng/StudentTrainingProgram.do)

Students are also encouraged to explore KIST School’s research areas and faculty profiles before selecting a research field that aligns with their academic interests and future career goals.

8. Important Notes

This is a
full-time internship program
. Participants must maintain their enrollment status at their current university throughout the internship period.

As the internship is conducted on a full-time basis, students are advised not to enroll in courses at their home university during the internship.

If a student graduates before or during the internship and can no longer maintain student status, the internship offer may be withdrawn or the internship may be terminated.

If any information provided in the application changes after submission, students must notify the KIST Academic Affairs Team. Failure to report such changes may result in the cancellation of acceptance or termination of the internship.
