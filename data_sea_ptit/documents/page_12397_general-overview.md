## Nội dung chi tiết:

Overview

The Faculty of Electronics Engineering I at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT) is widely recognized as one of the leading institutions for Electronic Engineering education in Vietnam. It serves not only as a hub for knowledge acquisition but also as an environment that fosters comprehensive student development, preparing graduates to adapt to the rapid advancements in the technological world, particularly in the fields of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Control and Automation Engineering, and Robotics.
At the
Faculty of Electronics Engineering I
, students study and conduct research under the guidance of a highly experienced faculty. 100% of the teaching staff hold a master’s degree or higher, including numerous Associate Professors and Ph.Ds. Notably, the faculty also receives academic mentorship from leading professors and experts from universities in Japan, Australia, and Europe.
Furthermore, students are not only equipped with foundational knowledge in electronics and automation but are also trained to conduct research in modern laboratories, such as the Rang Dong Lab (IoT & Embedded Systems), the Electronic Design Automation (EDA) Lab, and the Electronics and Intelligent Control (EIC) Lab.
These laboratories are equipped with advanced systems provided by world-leading partners in electronics, microchips, and automation, including ARM, Synopsys, Keysight, Intel, Xilinx, STMicroelectronics, Mentor, Altium, Siemens, Cadence, Dobot, and Hiwonder. The facilities feature development kits for embedded systems, digital systems, and IoT; high-precision measurement and control devices; specialized circuit and microchip design software; industrial robotic arms; Autonomous Guided Vehicles (AGVs); and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs).

Vision

Vision to 2030
By 2030, the Faculty of Electronics Engineering I aims to become a highly prestigious education and research institution with a recognized impact both domestically and regionally. The Faculty will focus on expanding and developing the following key areas:
Electrical & Electronic Engineering
Semiconductor & IC Design
Embedded Systems & IoT
Intelligent Control & Automation
Robotics, UAVs & Intelligent Autonomous Systems
The Faculty operates on a model that tightly integrates education, research, and practical demands, utilizing graduate quality and real-world applicability as its core metrics. On this foundation, the Faculty will accelerate curriculum innovation, enhance hands-on practical experiences, and implement real-world projects. Concurrently, it will expand collaborations with domestic and international enterprises and partners, while robustly driving innovation in education, research, and technology transfer. This comprehensive approach aims to elevate competitiveness and contribute effectively to the development of the Institute and the broader technology sector.

Mission

To build the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 into a high-quality and comprehensive technical human resource training unit, meeting the development needs of society in the field of Electricity – Electronics – Automation, with core orientations such as semiconductor microchips, embedded systems, control – automation and robotics; A quality, dynamic and professional learning and research environment that helps students maximize their own creative capacity; A scientific research, technology and knowledge transfer center in the top tier of the nation, region and the world; A reliable address in research and training cooperation with domestic and foreign enterprises.

Functions and Duties

Functions

The Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 is a training unit under the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (hereinafter referred to as the Institute), with the function of organizing training and scientific research in the field of Electricity – Electronics – Automation, including specialized directions such as semiconductor microchips, embedded systems, control – automation and robotics, contributing to providing high-quality human resources to serve socio-economic development and high-tech industry.
Duties
Organize the management and teaching of modules in the field of Electricity – Electronics – Automation for training systems and levels of the Institute;
Build plans and deploy training activities according to the general orientation, focusing on updating new technologies such as semiconductor microchips, intelligent systems and robotics;
Organize the implementation of scientific and technological activities; promote research in the fields of microchips, control, automation, robots; strengthen international cooperation and enterprise linkages;
Implement political, ideological, ethical and lifestyle education for officials, lecturers and learners; Build and develop the teaching staff towards specialized directions; expand training occupations; invest in facilities and laboratories to serve training and research in spearhead fields;
Manage officials, lecturers and learners; implement evaluation according to regulations; propose organizational and personnel plans of the faculty;
Manage and effectively use allocated assets and funds; implement the reporting regime and take responsibility for the accuracy of data;
Deploy guidelines, policies and regulations of higher authorities; coordinate with mass organizations in organizing emulation, study and research movements; Perform other duties as assigned by the President of the Institute.
