# SEMINAR ON “DOMAIN-SPECIFIC AI: FROM MATERIALS TO SYSTEM INTEGRATION AND OPPORTUNITIES FOR VIETNAM”

## Nội dung chi tiết:

On the afternoon of September 23, 2026, the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT) organized a seminar entitled
“Domain-Specific AI: From Materials to System Integration and Opportunities for Vietnam”
at the Auditorium of Building A2. The event brought together PTIT leaders, representatives of various departments and units, lecturers, researchers, graduate students, and undergraduate students.

An overview of the seminar held at the Auditorium of Building A2, Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology.

The seminar featured
Prof. Pham Nam Hai from the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Institute of Science Tokyo
, who shared in-depth insights into the intersection of materials science, integrated circuits, artificial intelligence, and electronic technologies. The discussion also explored research and development (R&D), the commercialization of high-tech products, and emerging opportunities for Vietnamese professionals in advanced technology fields.

From Materials to System Integration

During the seminar, Prof. Pham Nam Hai discussed the development of technologies from fundamental materials to integrated circuits and system-level integration, highlighting the connections between these fields in the advancement of modern electronic technologies.

Prof. Pham Nam Hai delivering his presentation at the seminar.

Beyond technical topics, the seminar also addressed research and development (R&D), the commercialization of high-tech products, and opportunities for Vietnamese human resources amid rapid developments in emerging technology sectors.

The speaker’s insights provided practical perspectives on the relationship between scientific research, technological development, and the application of research outcomes. These discussions also suggested potential directions for education and research in electronics, integrated circuits, and artificial intelligence.

Prof. Pham Nam Hai taking a commemorative photograph with PTIT representatives.

Academic Networking and Knowledge Exchange

In addition to the presentation, the seminar provided an interactive platform for lecturers and students to engage directly with the speaker, raise questions, and exchange ideas on research, academic pursuits, and career development in electronics, integrated circuits, and artificial intelligence.The active participation of lecturers, researchers, graduate students, and undergraduate students contributed to an open and engaging academic atmosphere. The event offered participants valuable opportunities to gain practical insights into emerging technological trends and future workforce demands.

Support and Collaboration

The seminar received valuable support from partner organizations and sponsors, whose contributions helped facilitate the event and strengthen connections between education, scientific research, and real-world technological development.

Representatives of partner organizations and sponsors supporting the seminar.

Students attending and following the seminar presentations.

Through meaningful discussions and exchanges with the invited expert, the seminar
“Domain-Specific AI: From Materials to System Integration and Opportunities for Vietnam”
helped broaden the perspectives of lecturers and students on technological advancements. The event also contributed to strengthening the links between education, research, and practical applications, particularly in the fields of electronics, integrated circuits, and artificial intelligence.
