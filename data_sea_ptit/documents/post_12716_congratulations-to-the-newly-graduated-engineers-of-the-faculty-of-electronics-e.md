## Nội dung chi tiết:

🎓 **CONGRATULATIONS TO THE NEWLY GRADUATED ENGINEERS OF THE FACULTY OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING 1 – CLASS OF 2026 (FIRST GRADUATION CEREMONY)** 🎓

We would like to extend our warmest congratulations to the newly graduated engineers of the **Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 (FEEE1)** who officially completed their studies and graduated in the first graduation ceremony of 2026, held on **May 23, 2026**.

After years of learning, striving, and growing under the roof of PTIT, you have successfully reached a significant milestone in your academic and professional journey. Your engineering degree is not only the result of countless hours spent in classrooms, laboratories, capstone projects, assignments, and deadline-driven nights, but also a testament to your perseverance, dedication, and determination to excel.

As you embark on a new chapter, we encourage you to continue nurturing your passion for **electronics, semiconductor technology, embedded systems, telecommunications, automation, and emerging technologies**. May you carry forward the spirit and traditions of FEEE1, confidently pursue your professional aspirations, and make meaningful contributions to industry, research, and society.

Your graduation marks not an ending, but the beginning of new opportunities, challenges, and achievements. We are proud of what you have accomplished and look forward to witnessing your future successes.

Once again, congratulations to all newly graduated engineers of the **Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 – Class of 2026 (First Graduation Ceremony)**. We wish you good health, happiness, and continued success in all your future endeavors.

🌟 **Proud of Today – Ready for Tomorrow!**
