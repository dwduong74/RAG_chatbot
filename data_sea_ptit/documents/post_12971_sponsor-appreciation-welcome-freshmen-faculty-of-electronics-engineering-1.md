## Nội dung chi tiết:

One journey – Countless pieces – Coming together to create a complete program!

Another milestone has officially come to an end, leaving us with countless unforgettable memories. The recent Freshman Welcome Night hosted by the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 was not only a formal occasion, but also a wonderful beginning to the vibrant years of youth awaiting the D26 students at the beloved PTIT home.

First and foremost, the Organizing Committee would like to express our deepest gratitude to the University Board of Management, distinguished lecturers, and alumni of the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1. The sincere and heartfelt sharing during the
“Take-off Essentials”
session served as a guiding light, equipping our freshmen with confidence and valuable insights as they embark on a new chapter.

In particular, we would like to extend our sincere appreciation to our valued Sponsors for honoring outstanding individuals and for their invaluable support and companionship:

VNPT Technology

FPT Software Co., Ltd.

EETIUM Technology Joint Stock Company

SK Vietnam Human Resources Co., Ltd.

WBS TRAINING VIETNAM

The meaningful support and generous contributions from our valued sponsors have played an essential role in bringing the program’s ideas to life in the most thoughtful and successful way.

Finally, the Organizing Committee would like to express our heartfelt thanks to the D26 freshmen. Your enthusiastic participation, vibrant energy, and talented performances from each class formed the central piece of the picture that brought
“The Mosaic”
to life. You are the perfect pieces that have contributed to the success of the program today.

Once again, the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 would like to extend our sincere gratitude to everyone and look forward to seeing you again in many exciting journeys to come!
