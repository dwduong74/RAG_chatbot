# Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 Congratulates Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien and Dr. Le Minh Tuan on Their New Appointments

- **Nguồn bài viết**: [https://sea.ptit.edu.vn/en/2026/10/19/13206/](https://sea.ptit.edu.vn/en/2026/10/19/13206/)
- **Ngày đăng**: 2026-10-19T21:33:53
- **Chuyên mục**: News - Announcements - Events

## Nội dung chi tiết:

The Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 extends its warmest congratulations to Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien and Dr. Le Minh Tuan on their appointments to new leadership positions, taking on important responsibilities in academic management, education, and professional development at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT).

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien and Dr. Le Minh Tuan at the ceremony announcing and presenting their new appointment decisions.

Accordingly,
Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien has been appointed Vice Dean of the Faculty of Graduate Studies, while Dr. Le Minh Tuan has been appointed Head of the Department of Computer Electronics.

Throughout their careers at PTIT, both faculty members have consistently demonstrated a strong sense of responsibility and dedication to teaching, education, scientific research, and their assigned professional duties. Their contributions have played an important role in advancing academic programs, research activities, and the overall development of the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 and PTIT.

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien – Vice Dean of the Faculty of Graduate Studies

In his new role as Vice Dean of the Faculty of Graduate Studies, Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien will continue to leverage his academic expertise and management experience to strengthen postgraduate education, enhance the quality of academic programs and scientific research, and promote the development of graduate education at PTIT.

Dr. Le Minh Tuan – Head of the Department of Computer Electronics

As Head of the Department of Computer Electronics, Dr. Le Minh Tuan will assume key responsibilities in academic management, education, and departmental development. Working closely with fellow faculty members, he will continue to foster a strong academic and research environment within the Department.

These new appointments mark significant milestones in the professional careers of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien and Dr. Le Minh Tuan. They also bring new responsibilities and expectations in academic leadership, education, and professional development.

With their solid academic backgrounds, extensive professional experience, and strong commitment to their responsibilities, the Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 is confident that both faculty members will continue to build upon their achievements, successfully fulfill their duties in their new positions, and make further valuable contributions to the development of the Faculty and PTIT.

The Faculty of Electronics Engineering 1 once again extends its sincere congratulations to Assoc. Prof. Dr. Pham Minh Trien and Dr. Le Minh Tuan, wishing them good health, continued enthusiasm, and every success in their new roles.
