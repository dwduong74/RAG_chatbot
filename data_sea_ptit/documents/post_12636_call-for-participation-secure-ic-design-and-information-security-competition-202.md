## Nội dung chi tiết:

🔐
CALL FOR PARTICIPATION: “SECURE IC DESIGN AND INFORMATION SECURITY COMPETITION 2026

To foster an academic environment that encourages research, innovation, and the development of high-quality talent in the semiconductor industry, the
Academy of Cryptography Techniques (ACT)
is organizing the
Secure IC Design and Information Security Competition 2026
for university students across Vietnam.

📌
Competition Information

Organizer:
Academy of Cryptography Techniques (ACT)

Duration:
May 2026 – October 2026

Eligibility:
Undergraduate students from universities and academies nationwide who have ideas, projects, or research topics related to integrated circuit (IC) design.

Participation Format:
Individual or team (up to 5 members).

🎯
Competition Objectives

Encourage students to conduct research, innovate, and apply specialized knowledge to real-world challenges.

Identify and nurture future talents in secure IC design and hardware security.

Provide participants with hands-on experience throughout the entire IC design flow, from concept development, logic design, simulation, and verification to final product implementation.

💡
Competition Themes

Projects may focus on areas such as:

Hardware Security

Information Security

Secure Semiconductor and IC Design

High-Performance and Reliable Hardware Systems

The competition offers an excellent opportunity for students interested in semiconductor technology, IC design, cybersecurity, and hardware security to showcase their creativity, technical expertise, and innovative solutions.
