# 📸 RECAP | D26 – FIRST STEPS, A MEMORABLE BEGINNING

- **Nguồn bài viết**: [https://sea.ptit.edu.vn/en/2026/09/13/%f0%9f%93%b8-recap-d26-first-steps-a-memorable-beginning/](https://sea.ptit.edu.vn/en/2026/09/13/%f0%9f%93%b8-recap-d26-first-steps-a-memorable-beginning/)

## Nội dung chi tiết:

Over the two days of
August 22 and 23
, the
Faculty Youth Union of Electronics Engineering 1
warmly welcomed the new faces of
D26
in the vibrant atmosphere of the new academic year at PTIT. From the first moments filled with a little shyness and hesitation, the freshmen gradually became more open and confident, beginning to experience the new home where they will spend the coming years.

💙
On August 22
, we welcomed the first freshmen from the
Integrated Bachelor’s–Master’s Program in Electronics Engineering
, as well as
UAV and Autonomous Mobile Robotics
. On August 23, the atmosphere continued to grow more vibrant with the presence of students from
Semiconductor Microchip Technology
and
Control and Automation Engineering
in the morning, followed by
Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technology
in the afternoon. Throughout the conversations, students actively raised questions about their majors, specializations, career orientations, and future opportunities, showing their seriousness and eagerness to explore the path they have chosen.

✨ Yet, the two days of enrollment were about more than simply discussing academic programs. A few fun minigames, thoughtful keychains and stickers, along with warm and friendly conversations, gradually brought D26 closer to lecturers and senior members of the Faculty Youth Union. From being strangers at first, the students soon felt comfortable sharing their interests, personalities, and personal stories — making their first day at
Booth No. 2 of the Faculty
a warm and memorable experience.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Behind those smiles were also the heartfelt emotions of parents: a little concern, a little nervousness, but above all, immense pride as they witnessed their children taking their first confident steps through the doors of university and beginning to write their own stories.

🌧️ Although the weather was somewhat unpredictable, even the rain could not dampen D26’s excitement. The two days of enrollment came to an end with countless smiles, memorable encounters, and the dedicated support of lecturers and senior members of the
Faculty Youth Union of Electronics Engineering 1
.

💫
Welcome to D26 and the Faculty Youth Union of Electronics Engineering 1!
May you always carry the enthusiasm you showed today — be willing to learn, dare to explore, embrace new challenges, and have the courage to turn your years at PTIT into a journey you will always be proud of.

A new beginning has just begun — and the most beautiful moments are still ahead.
💙
