# Industry Partnership Activities

- **Ngày cập nhật**: 2026-06-17T16:18:43

## Nội dung chi tiết:

1. Overview

The Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 (FEE1) maintains and develops a network of close cooperation with domestic and foreign enterprises to enhance the quality of education, bridge the gap between theory and practice, and increase career opportunities for students.

Enterprise cooperation activities are implemented with the orientation of
aligning education with the demands of the labor market
, and they represent a crucial component of the Academy’s educational quality assurance system.

2. Enterprise Partners

FEE1 has established cooperative relationships with numerous enterprises across various fields:

Electrical & Electronic Engineering;

Semiconductor & IC Design;

Embedded Systems & IoT;

Intelligent Control & Automation;

Robotics, UAV & Autonomous Systems.

3. Forms of Cooperation

Cooperation between FEE1 and enterprises is implemented in diverse forms:

🔹 Internships and Recruitment

Enterprises accept interns

Coordinate in organizing job fairs

Recruit students upon graduation

🔹 Teaching and Training Participation

Industry experts participate in teaching specialized topics

Organizing seminars, workshops, and talk shows to share practical professional experience

🔹 Program Development and Review

Contributing feedback on training program content

Participating in advisory boards

Proposing necessary skills and competencies

🔹 Projects and Practical Activities

Students participate in real-world projects at enterprises

Organizing competitions, hackathons, and project-based learning

Cooperating in research and technology transfer

4. The Role of Enterprises in Education

Enterprises play a vital role in the educational process by:

Contributing to defining the expected learning outcomes of training programs

Assessing student competencies and the quality of education

Participating in the curriculum improvement process

Supporting the development of a learning environment linked to reality

5. Opportunities for Students

Through this enterprise cooperation network, students of FEE1 have the opportunity to:

Participate in internships at reputable enterprises

Access a practical working environment

Develop professional and soft skills

Get employed immediately upon graduation

6. Typical Activities

FEE1 regularly organizes cooperation activities with enterprises such as:

Job Fairs

Specialized seminars with enterprises

Mentoring and career orientation programs

Professional competitions and real-world projects

Cooperation in building research laboratories

7. Cooperation Results

Enterprise cooperation activities have yielded positive results:

High employment rate for students after graduation

Students are assessed as having skills aligned with job requirements

Cooperative relationships with enterprises are increasingly expanding

8. Commitment to Development

The Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 is committed to:

Continuing to expand the network of enterprise partners

Enhancing the participation of enterprises in educational activities

Improving the quality of education linked to practice

Better meeting the demands of the labor market
